A new model is interesting. A repeatable improvement in your work is useful. Start the evaluation with one task you already do, and a clear definition of a good result.

## Choose one bounded task.

Try structuring source notes, reviewing a draft, or classifying documents. Keep sensitive information out of a service until you understand its access and data controls.

## Measure the whole workflow.

Include the time spent writing instructions, checking output, and correcting mistakes. A fast first draft that requires extensive repair may not save time.

## Keep a human checkpoint.

Decide which claims need sources and which actions require your approval. Save a small set of examples so the same workflow can be tested again when tools change.
